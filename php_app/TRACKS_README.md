**SANKALP 2027** is organized around **six multidisciplinary research tracks** addressing global challenges in sustainability, carbon neutrality, next-generation computing, smart cities, and circular economy.

## ⚡ Track 1: Sustainable Energy Systems and Decarbonization Technologies

### Focus & Description
Focuses on clean energy transitions, renewable generation, energy conservation, and carbon mitigation technologies. This track addresses the technical, operational, and policy aspects of moving toward a net-zero energy future.

### Key Sub-Themes & Topics
1. **Renewable Energy Systems:** Solar PV, CSP, wind turbine advancements, bioenergy, geothermal, and ocean energy systems.
2. **Energy Storage Technologies:** Battery chemistry (Li-ion, solid-state, flow batteries), thermal storage, and supercapacitors.
3. **Smart Grids and Microgrids:** Grid modernization, distributed energy resource management (DERMS), and bi-directional power flows.
4. **Hydrogen Energy Systems:** Green hydrogen production, fuel cells, hydrogen storage, safety protocols, and supply chains.
5. **Carbon Capture and Emission Reduction:** CCUS (Carbon Capture, Utilization, and Storage), direct air capture, and industrial flue-gas treatment.
6. **Net-Zero Energy Solutions:** Hybrid generation systems, power electronics converters, and zero-emission microgrid architectures.

* **Target SDGs:** SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy), SDG 13 (Climate Action).

---

## 🏙️ Track 2: Smart and Sustainable Built Environments

### Focus & Description
Explores advancements in high-performance architecture, energy-efficient building envelopes, urban informatics, and digital twins for intelligent civil infrastructure.

### Key Sub-Themes & Topics
1. **Intelligent Buildings:** Smart HVAC, IoT sensor integration, occupancy-based building automation, and cognitive structures.
2. **Green and Net-Zero Buildings:** Passive design strategies, bio-climatic architecture, net-zero water and energy buildings, and LEED/GRIHA certifications.
3. **Smart Cities and Smart Districts:** Urban sensing networks, municipal resource optimization, smart street lighting, and spatial analytics.
4. **Digital Twins for Infrastructure:** BIM-to-Digital-Twin workflows, real-time lifecycle monitoring, and simulation of municipal assets.
5. **Sustainable Urban Development:** Eco-friendly building materials, low-carbon geopolymer concrete, urban heat island mitigation, and spatial zoning.
6. **Building Energy Management Systems (BEMS):** Predictive demand response, automated energy auditing, and peak load shaving.

* **Target SDGs:** SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure), SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities).

---

## ♻️ Track 3: Sustainable Manufacturing and Circular Economy

### Focus & Description
Dedicated to cleaner production methods, closed-loop supply chains, waste valorization, and Industry 5.0 concepts that place environmental stewardship and human-centric automation at the forefront.

### Key Sub-Themes & Topics
1. **Sustainable Manufacturing Technologies:** Green machining, minimum quantity lubrication (MQL), energy-efficient casting, and additive manufacturing for lightweighting.
2. **Industry 5.0 Applications:** Human-robot collaboration (Cobots), smart factories, cyber-physical production, and ethical automation.
3. **Resource-Efficient Production:** Lean, clean, and green manufacturing frameworks; material input minimization; and process intensification.
4. **Circular Economy Models:** Cradle-to-cradle lifecycle engineering, product remanufacturing, refurbishment, and industrial symbiosis.
5. **Recycling and Waste Valorization:** Electronic waste (e-waste) processing, plastics upcycling, agro-industrial waste recovery, and metallurgical slag reuse.
6. **Sustainable Supply Chains:** Carbon-footprint tracking in logistics, reverse logistics, and blockchain for ethical material provenance.

* **Target SDGs:** SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure), SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production).

---

## 🤖 Track 4: Artificial Intelligence and Digital Technologies for Sustainability

### Focus & Description
Examines how advanced computing paradigms—machine learning, big data analytics, cloud-native systems, and Edge IoT—can be leveraged to solve complex environmental, resource, and engineering challenges.

### Key Sub-Themes & Topics
1. **AI and Machine Learning Applications:** Deep learning, reinforcement learning, and physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) applied to sustainability and scientific discovery.
2. **Big Data Analytics:** Planetary-scale geospatial data analysis, streaming sensor analytics, and predictive modeling for climate and natural resources.
3. **Internet of Things (IoT):** Ultra-low-power environmental sensor networks, edge intelligence, and secure wireless sensor nodes.
4. **Image Processing and Cloud Computing:** Satellite imagery analysis for deforestation/crop monitoring, computer vision for defect detection, and scalable cloud-based ML pipelines.
5. **Digital Transformation for Sustainability:** Enterprise ESG data platforms, smart metering analytics, and automated compliance auditing.
6. **Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS):** Resilient control loops, digital security for critical environmental infrastructure, and automated robotic interventions.

* **Target SDGs:** SDG 8 (Decent Work & Economic Growth), SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure).

---

## 🌿 Track 5: Climate Change, Environmental Management and Resource Sustainability

### Focus & Description
Focuses on ecological conservation, pollution monitoring, circular water management, soil remediation, and technological interventions to adapt to and mitigate climate variability.

### Key Sub-Themes & Topics
1. **Climate Change Mitigation and Adaptation:** Regional climate models, carbon sequestration in forestry and soils, and vulnerable ecosystem risk management.
2. **Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA):** Advanced life cycle assessment (LCA), ecological risk indices, and biodiversity conservation modeling.
3. **Air and Water Quality Management:** Real-time particulate matter (PM2.5/PM10) sensing, wastewater recycling, membrane bioreactors, and point-of-use water purification.
4. **Natural Resource Conservation:** Watershed management, groundwater replenishment techniques, and forest cover monitoring.
5. **Sustainable Agriculture Systems:** Precision farming, drone-assisted crop health monitoring, smart irrigation schedules, and bio-pesticides.
6. **Environmental Monitoring Technologies:** Remote sensing, drones/UAVs for environmental surveillance, and autonomous aquatic monitoring probes.

* **Target SDGs:** SDG 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation), SDG 13 (Climate Action), SDG 15 (Life on Land).

---

## 🚆 Track 6: Sustainable Transportation and Resilient Infrastructure Systems

### Focus & Description
Covers clean vehicular propulsion, connected vehicle ecosystems, multi-modal public transit, disaster mitigation engineering, and the resilience of critical transport networks.

### Key Sub-Themes & Topics
1. **Smart Transportation Systems:** Intelligent traffic management systems (ITMS), dynamic route optimization, and urban congestion pricing models.
2. **Electric and Autonomous Vehicles:** EV powertrain engineering, battery swapping infrastructure, wireless charging roads, and Level 3–5 autonomous driving safety.
3. **Intelligent Road Infrastructure:** Solar-harvesting pavements, self-healing asphalt, and vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I/V2X) communication.
4. **Structural Health Monitoring (SHM):** Vibration sensing, acoustic emission testing, fiber-optic strain sensing for bridges, tunnels, and railway viaducts.
5. **Infrastructure Resilience:** Climate-proofing coastal, highway, and rail transport; seismic isolation systems; and flood defense structures.
6. **Disaster-Resistant Engineering Systems:** Rapid post-disaster structural assessments, emergency transit rerouting algorithms, and retrofitting heritage structures.

* **Target SDGs:** SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure), SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities).

## Submission Guidelines & Important Dates

### Types of Submissions Accepted
- **Full Research Papers:** 6 to 8 pages describing original, unpublished theoretical or experimental investigations.
- **Short / Practitioner Papers:** 4 to 5 pages covering industrial implementations, case studies, or applied engineering prototypes.
- **Poster Presentations:** Extended abstracts describing high-impact ongoing or exploratory research.

### Important Deadlines
- **Full Paper Submission Deadline:** 30 September 2026
- **Notification of Acceptance:** 31 October 2026
- **Camera-Ready Submission Deadline:** 20 November 2026
- **Early Bird Registration Deadline:** 15 November 2026
- **Conference Presentation Dates:** 15–16 February 2027

### How to Submit
1. Navigate to the online conference platform at `http://localhost:8080/` (or production URL).
2. Click **Submit Paper** on any track card to open the track-specific modal.
3. Select your intended track and upload your blinded manuscript PDF following IEEE/Springer formatting conventions.
