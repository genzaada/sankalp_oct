Author Submission & Manuscript Preparation Guidelines (Provisional)
Important Notice for Authors: These guidelines provide provisional formatting, ethical, and structural criteria for manuscripts intended for ICAIESF 2027. Submissions are planned to be processed through the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT). This document outlines standard academic conference conventions and does not represent a proprietary publisher template, official publisher endorsement, or guarantee of paper acceptance, page limits, or indexing. Official templates, final page specifications, and formal submission links will be announced upon formal activation.
Submission Portal Status: The conference-specific Microsoft CMT submission portal has not yet been activated and submissions are not currently open. Official submission links, deadlines, and publishing templates will be announced upon formal activation.

1. General Paper Submission Requirements

Authors are invited to submit original, previously unpublished research papers, practical case studies, and comprehensive review papers addressing one of the conference's six official thematic tracks. Submissions must present significant technical contributions, sound methodology, rigorous validation, and clear relevance to intelligent engineering, sustainable mobility, advanced materials, energy systems, or multidisciplinary technologies.

  • Language: All manuscripts must be written in formal academic English. Clear phrasing, grammatical accuracy, and standard technical terminology are required.
  • Originality: Papers must report original research that has not been published elsewhere and is not under simultaneous consideration by any other journal, conference, or workshop.
  • Page Limits (Provisional): Full research manuscripts should typically be between 6 to 8 pages in standard two-column format, including all figures, tables, formulas, acknowledgments, and bibliographic references, subject to final publisher proceedings guidelines.
  • File Format: Submissions must be prepared in Portable Document Format (.pdf). Ensure all fonts are embedded and vector assets are intact prior to submission.

2. Manuscript Formatting Instructions

Authors should follow standard academic two-column formatting conventions to facilitate uniform peer review:

Format Element Standard Specification
Paper Size & Layout Standard A4 (210 mm × 297 mm), Two-column format (column width: ~88 mm, gutter: ~5 mm).
Margins Top: 19 mm (0.75 in), Bottom: 25 mm (1.0 in), Left/Right: 16 mm (0.63 in).
Typeface (Font) Times New Roman (or comparable standard serif font). Body text: 10 pt with standard line spacing.
Section Headings Level 1: 10 pt, Bold, Small Caps or All Caps, centered or left-aligned. Level 2: 10 pt, Italic or Bold, left-aligned.

3. Paper Title & Author Information (Provisional Guidance)

To uphold standards of objective academic evaluation, submissions will undergo a formal peer-review process. Review protocols and manuscript anonymization guidelines will be specified upon official CFP announcement.

  • Title: The title should be concise, informative, and indicative of the paper's core contributions. Avoid acronyms and non-standard symbols in the title.
  • Author Anonymization: For the initial review submission, do NOT include author names, affiliations, email addresses, institutional logos, or explicit postal details anywhere in the manuscript.
  • Self-Citations: Refer to your own previous work in the third person (e.g., "Smith et al. [3] previously demonstrated..." instead of "In our previous work [3]...").
  • Camera-Ready Preparation: Author names, institutional affiliations, corresponding email addresses, and biographical notes will be added only after formal acceptance.

4. Abstract & Keywords

  • Abstract: Every paper must include a self-contained, standalone abstract of 150 to 250 words. The abstract should concisely outline the research problem, proposed methodology, key experimental findings, and core conclusions. Avoid mathematical equations, citations, or undefined abbreviations.
  • Keywords: Provide 4 to 6 representative keywords separated by commas directly beneath the abstract. Keywords should reflect key methodologies, domains, and core technologies (e.g., Electric Vehicles, Intelligent Systems, Thermal Engineering, Industry 5.0).

5. Main Manuscript Structure

Manuscripts should be logically structured following standard academic publishing conventions:

  1. Introduction: State the problem statement, industrial or societal significance, research objectives, and concise summary of contributions.
  2. Related Work: Survey relevant state-of-the-art literature, critically analyzing gaps that the proposed work addresses.
  3. Proposed Methodology / System Architecture: Provide comprehensive technical descriptions, algorithms, mathematical formulations, and system designs with sufficient detail for reproducibility.
  4. Experimental Setup & Results: Describe datasets, experimental baselines, hardware/simulation parameters, quantitative performance metrics, and empirical findings.
  5. Discussion: Interpret results, analyze limitations, and evaluate practical feasibility in real-world scenarios.
  6. Conclusion & Future Scope: Summarize findings and highlight promising directions for continued investigation.
  7. References: Comprehensive and properly cited bibliographic entries.

6. Figures, Tables, and Equations

  • Figures: All graphs, diagrams, and photos must have high visual clarity (recommended 300 DPI resolution, provisional subject to official publisher templates). Captions must appear centered directly below the figure (e.g., "Figure 1: Schematic diagram of the proposed system.").
  • Tables: Tables must be numbered consecutively with descriptive titles placed directly above the table (e.g., "Table 1: Performance comparison across baseline models."). Use clean, standard borders without vertical grid clutters.
  • Equations: Mathematical expressions must be typeset using standard equation editors, centered on their own lines, and numbered consecutively with Arabic numerals in parentheses aligned to the right margin:
    E = mc²                                                                                                           (1)

7. Citation and Reference Formatting

Citations should adhere to standard academic numbered reference conventions (provisionally IEEE numbered reference style, subject to final conference proceedings specifications). References must appear in the order in which they are first cited in text using bracketed numerals (e.g., "[1]", "[2, 3]").

  • Every cited source must appear in the reference list, and every reference listed must be cited in the text.
  • Include Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs) where available to assist citation verification.
  • Reference entries must include: Author names, article/book title, publication venue, volume, issue, page numbers, and year of publication.

8. Originality, Academic Integrity & Ethical Policies

  • All submissions must represent original intellectual contributions. Submissions must be free from plagiarism, duplicate publication, data fabrication, and improper appropriation of text or ideas.
  • Manuscripts are expected to uphold strict scholarly integrity standards in accordance with internationally accepted publication ethics.
  • Plagiarism, self-plagiarism, unauthorized reuse of figures or proprietary data, and fabricated content are strictly prohibited and subject to immediate desk rejection.

9. Ethical Publication Practices

  • Authorship: All individuals listed as authors must have made substantial intellectual contributions to the conception, design, execution, or interpretation of the reported study.
  • Generative AI Usage: AI-assisted tools may only be utilized for improving readability or language polishing. Generative AI tools cannot be listed as an author, and any substantial algorithmic assistance must be transparently disclosed.
  • Conflict of Interest: Authors must disclose any relevant financial, institutional, or personal conflicts of interest during the submission process.

10. Microsoft CMT Submission Workflow (General Guidance)

Paper submission and peer-review administration are planned to be managed via the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT):

  1. Account Setup: Visit the official Microsoft CMT portal (https://cmt3.research.microsoft.com). If you do not have an existing CMT account, click "Register" to create your free account using your institutional email address.
  2. Conference Portal Access: The specific ICAIESF 2027 conference submission portal is currently pending activation. When active, verified links will be provided on the conference website.
  3. Track Selection: Select the appropriate conference track from the six official technical tracks:
    • Track 1: Sustainable Automotive Mobility, Electric Vehicles and Intelligent Transportation Systems
    • Track 2: Intelligent Sustainable Manufacturing and Industry 5.0
    • Track 3: Smart and Sustainable Materials, Infrastructure and Advanced Engineering Systems
    • Track 4: Sustainable Energy Systems, Thermal Engineering and Renewable Energy Integration
    • Track 5: Artificial Intelligence and Digital Technologies for Intelligent Engineering
    • Track 6: Environmental Sustainability, Climate Resilience and Circular Economy
  4. Metadata Entry: Enter the paper title, structured abstract, primary keywords, and select subject areas corresponding to your manuscript.
  5. Author Declarations & Conflicts: Declare any institutional conflicts of interest (COIs) to ensure unbiased reviewer assignment.
  6. Upload Manuscript: Upload the manuscript in PDF format in accordance with author instructions. Verify that all figures and text render properly.
  7. Acknowledgment: Confirm submission. CMT will generate a unique Submission ID and send an automated acknowledgment email. Keep this Submission ID for all future correspondence.
